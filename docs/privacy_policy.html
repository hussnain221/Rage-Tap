Privacy Policy for Rage Tap

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Rage Tap ("the game", "the application") is an offline mobile arcade skill game published by StormLogix ("we", "us", "our"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy discloses our information practices.

1. Information We Collect

Rage Tap does not collect, store on servers, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
  • The game operates completely offline and makes zero network requests during gameplay.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The game stores minimal operational data exclusively on your device's local storage (Android SharedPreferences):

  • Game Records: High score, total distance, and run count.
  • Settings: Sound effects and vibration/haptics preferences.
  • Teaching Hints: One-time tutorial display flags (so teaching banners only appear on first encounter).

This data never leaves your device. It is completely deleted when you uninstall the application.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

This release of Rage Tap contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and no third-party analytics libraries. No user data is sent to external services.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Rage Tap is suitable for general audiences. Because our application does not collect, store, or transmit personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Permissions

Rage Tap requires zero sensitive permissions. The only hardware feature utilized is device vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE) for jump and collision feedback, which can be disabled at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

6. Changes to this Policy

If future updates introduce features that interact with external services, this Privacy Policy and Google Play Data Safety forms will be updated accordingly prior to release.
