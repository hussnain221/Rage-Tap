# Privacy Policy for Rage Tap

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

**Rage Tap** ("the game", "the app") is an offline mobile arcade game developed and published by **StormLogix** ("we", "us", "our"). 

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and data handling.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
**Rage Tap does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any personal information.**
- We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or IP address.
- The game does not connect to the internet during active gameplay.

### 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
To provide core game functionality, Rage Tap saves small operational data exclusively on your device's local storage:
- **Best score & run statistics** (to display your high score).
- **Player settings** (sound effects toggle and vibration/haptics toggle).
- **One-time tutorial hint flags** (to ensure introductory teaching banners appear only once).

**This data never leaves your device.** It is stored locally using standard Android storage (`SharedPreferences`) and is permanently erased when you uninstall the game or clear app storage.

### 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising
- This release of Rage Tap contains **no third-party advertising SDKs**, no external tracking scripts, and no analytics frameworks.
- No personal or behavioral data is shared with any third party.

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Rage Tap is designed as a family-safe arcade skill game. Because the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from any user, it fully complies with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

### 5. Device Permissions
Rage Tap requires **no sensitive device permissions**.
- No access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or Fine/Coarse Location.
- The only system feature used is standard device vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`) to provide tactile haptic feedback during jumps and collisions, which can be turned off in the in-game Settings.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If a future update introduces features that interact with external services (such as optional cloud leaderboards or opt-in rewarded ads), this Privacy Policy will be revised prior to publication, and Google Play Data Safety declarations will be updated accordingly.
